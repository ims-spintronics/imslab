Opportunities

Join our team and explore research openings in experimental spintronics and intelligent devices.

Senior Research Fellow / Research Associate / Project Officer

Open Until Filled

Research positions in experimental spintronics are available. The appointments are initially for one year with a possibility of renewal for another year.

Preferred candidate experience (in at least two of the following areas):
  • Resistive switching memory materials and device
  • Neuromorphic computing
  • Spin orbit torques

A qualified PhD degree in Physics, Engineering, or Material Science is required for the Research Fellow position. A qualified Master's degree in a similar discipline is required for the Research Associate position, while a first degree holder can be considered for the Project Officer position. Only motivated candidates with team spirit and an ability for independent work will be considered.

How to Apply:

Interested applicants please send your curriculum vitae, full list of publications, and contact details for references to Dr. Wen Siang Lew at wensiang@ntu.edu.sg.

PhD Scholarships

Full Funding Available

PhD research opportunities are available at the Spintronics Device Laboratory, School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (Physics Division), Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Students will have the opportunity to work on key research topics including non-volatile memory devices, spintronics, and ReRAM devices.

Key Requirements & Guidelines:
  • Bachelor's degree in Physics, Engineering, or Materials Science
  • Proficiency in spoken and written English is a must
  • GRE & TOEFL required (or pass NTU's EPT & TPT exams)

Several full scholarships are available for qualified students. Additional special funding programs include:

Application Links

• Graduate Scholarships: NTU Graduate Scholarships

• Singapore Citizens & PRs (EDB-IPP): Support available under the Industrial Postgraduate Programme. Details on EDB-IPP

• SINGA Award: International students can apply online at SINGA Scholarship Portal
