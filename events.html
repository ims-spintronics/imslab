Consortium Milestone

Singapore Spintronics Consortium (SG-SPIN) MOU Signing

Date: 5th December 2014

MOU signing ceremony at NUS Shaw Foundation Alumni House (5 Dec 2014).

Front row: Mr Russell Tham, President, Applied Materials South East Asia; Mr Rajesh Nair, Vice President, GLOBALFOUNDRIES Singapore; Prof Barry Halliwell, Deputy President (Research & Technology), NUS; Dr Lim Jui, CEO NTUitive of NTU and Mr Patrick Chang, Director, Delta Electronics International (Singapore). Back row: Prof Ding Jun (NUS); Prof Teo Kie Leong (NUS); Prof Adeyeye Adekunle (NUS); Mr George Loh, Program Director of NRF ; Prof Low Teck Seng, CEO of NRF; Prof Wu Yihong (NUS); Irene Cheong, Director ILO of NUS;Mr David Toh, CTO NTUitive of NTU; Prof Lew Wen Siang (NTU).

The event brought together executive leadership, key researchers, and principal investigators from NTU, NUS, A*STAR, and industry partners to push spintronics innovation forward.

Academic Workshop

1st Workshop on Non-Volatile Logic and Magnetic Devices

Date: 1st August 2013

Organized as part of the flagship research programme "Magnetic logic and memory integrated circuit devices", gathering regional researchers to discuss spin transport, skyrmion spin textures, and logic-in-memory architectures.

Workshop Photo Highlights

09:45 AM - Arrival
  • Prof. Ding Jun (NUS)
  • Assoc. Prof. Mansoor (NUS)
  • Asst. Prof. Quek Su Ying (IHPC-NUS)
  • Dr. Tan Seng Ghee (DSI)
  • Asst. Prof. Yu Hao (NTU)
10:00 AM - Opening

Welcome address by workshop host, Asst. Prof. Lew Wen Siang.

10:15 AM - Keynote

Assoc. Prof. Mansoor on "Topological Hall Effect in Vortex and Skyrmion Spin Textures".

02:00 PM - Research Talk

Mr. Indra Purnama presented on “Coupled Domain Wall Systems”.

Official group photo of participants at the conclusion of the workshop.
