Equipment & Facilities

State-of-the-art thin film deposition, micro/nano-fabrication, electrical probing, and magnetic characterisation systems.

Deposition

ULVAC ENTRON-EX 300 mm PVD System

High-throughput semiconductor deposition for metal interconnects, patterning masks, electrodes, and functional films.

Deposition

AJA 8–Target UHV MRAM Magnetron Sputtering System

Ultra-high vacuum magnetron deposition system optimized for MRAM and spintronic thin-film stacks.

Deposition

Picosun 200 mm Atomic Layer Deposition Tool

Atomic-scale precision deposition for ultra-thin dielectrics, passivation, and barrier layers with total conformality.

Lithography

Raith e-Line Electron Beam Lithography

High-resolution pattern generator for ultra-fine nanoscale device patterning and prototype fabrication.

Lithography

Raith Voyager Electron Beam Lithography

High-speed, scalable e-beam lithography engineered for spintronic devices, photonics, and sub-10 nm structures.

Lithography

Suss Microtec MJB4 Mask Aligner

Precision optical alignment lithography tool for micro-scale contact printing and multi-level mask patterning.

Etching

AJA Ion Milling System with Hiden SIMS Detector

Argon ion beam milling equipped with inline secondary ion mass spectrometry for endpoint detection in magnetic stacks.

Etch & CVD

Plasma Therm 200 mm Dual Chamber ICP-RIE and PECVD System

Dual-chamber tool supporting Inductively Coupled Plasma Reactive Ion Etching (ICP-RIE) and Plasma-Enhanced Chemical Vapor Deposition (PECVD).

RF Probing

FormFactor (Cascade) Microtech 40GHz RF Probe Station

High-frequency microwave probe station combined with magnetic field application setups for high-speed device dynamics.

Electrical

Keithley 4200A-SCS Semiconductor Parameter Analyzer

Comprehensive I-V, C-V, and ultra-fast pulsed characterization analyzer for memristive, spintronic, and neuromorphic devices.
