Equipment & Facilities

State-of-the-art film deposition, micro/nano-fabrication, and magnetic characterisation systems.

Deposition

AJA 8–Target UHV MRAM Magnetron Sputtering System

Ultra-high vacuum magnetron deposition system optimized for MRAM thin-film stacks.

Deposition

Custom-built 6-Target UHV Magnetron Sputtering System

Custom high-vacuum sputtering system engineered for specialized magnetic heterostructures.

Evaporation

Home-built Metal Growth UHV Evaporation System

Ultra-high vacuum thermal evaporation system customized for metallic thin-film growth.

Deposition

ULVAC ENTRON-EXX Sputtering System

Deposition processes for semiconductor fabrication for metal interconnects, patterning masks, electrodes, and other functional films.

Deposition

Atomic Layer Deposition Tool

For depositing critical dielectric, passivation, and barrier layers with total uniformity and zero defects.

Annealing

Custom-built In-Plane & Perpendicular Field Annealing High Vacuum System

High-vacuum thermal annealing system equipped with magnetic field orientation capabilities.

Nanolithography

Raith e-Line Electron Beam Lithography

High-resolution pattern generator for ultra-fine nanoscale device fabrication.

Photolithography

Suss Microtec MJB4 Mask Aligner

Precision optical alignment lithography tool for micro-scale contact patterns.

Etching

AJA Ion Milling System with Hiden SIMS Detector

Argon ion beam milling equipped with inline secondary ion mass spectrometry.

Deposition

Plasma Therm 200 mm Dual chamber ICO reactive Ion Etching and PECVD System

Performs both Reactive Ion Etching (RIE) and Plasma-Enhanced Chemical Vapor Deposition (PECVD) Precision photoresist and polymer coating systems.

Surface Cleaning

Quorum Technology Oxygen Plasma Barrel Etcher

RF plasma system for organic residue removal and surface activation prior to lithography.

RF Probing

Cascade Microtech 40GHz RF Probe Station

High-frequency microwave probe station combined with magnetic field application setups.

Electrical

Keithley 4200A-SCS Semiconductor Parameter Analyzer

Comprehensive I-V, C-V, and pulse characterization analyzer for memristive and logic devices.
