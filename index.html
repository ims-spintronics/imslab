Our Vision

To pioneer next-generation spintronic and intelligent memory technologies that power energy-efficient computing architectures and advanced biomedical sensing systems worldwide.

Our Mission

Bridging fundamental physics with industrial innovation through micro- and nano-fabrication, advanced device modeling, and interdisciplinary collaboration to deliver transformative technologies.

About The Laboratory

The Laboratory of Intelligent Materials and Systems (IMS Lab) at Nanyang Technological University Singapore , advances energy-efficient technologies in non-volatile memory, spintronics, and intelligent sensing. Our research spans device and circuit simulation, micro- and nano-fabrication, and precision magnetic/electronic characterisation, extending to MEMS/NEMS-based biomedical sensing.

Through strong industry and international collaborations, we bridge fundamental research and real-world technological innovation.

Core Research Areas

Spintronics

Non-Volatile Memory

Development and characterisation of next-generation spin-based non-volatile memory devices for energy-efficient computing architectures.

AI Hardware

Intelligent Systems

Integrating emerging memory devices into novel hardware for neuromorphic computing and intelligent sensor processing.

Bio-MEMS

Biomedical Sensing

Micro- and nano-electromechanical magnetic sensing systems engineered for biological diagnostic and biomedical applications.
