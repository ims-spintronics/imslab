Final Year Project (FYP)

PH4421 and PH4422: one- or two-semester research projects in experimental, theoretical and computational spintronics.

Course Overview

Registered students carry out a research project over one semester (PH4422, for students admitted in AY2024/25 and later) or two semesters (PH4421, for students admitted from AY2019/20 to AY2023/24). Students are assessed on supervisor evaluation, a written thesis, a viva examination and an oral presentation.

Our group welcomes undergraduate students every year for experimental, theoretical and modelling projects. Students train in our nano- and micro-fabrication laboratory and computational workstation facility, with close guidance from full-time researchers. We emphasise hands-on technical skills (mechanical, electrical and electronic) as much as research methodology and data analysis, so our graduates leave with strong analytical ability and practical laboratory experience. Our FYP students regularly co-author papers at international conferences and in peer-reviewed journals.

We have trained more than 50 FYP students. Our alumni now work in microelectronics, manufacturing engineering, defence research, materials research, ICT, education, civil defence and other fields.
